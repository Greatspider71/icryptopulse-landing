Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-08

What this Site collects

Nothing. The Site has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising.

What stays on your device

When you accept the Terms of Use, your browser stores a small note (in local storage) recording the Terms version you accepted, so you are not asked again on every visit. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Third parties

  • Hosting: the Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure its service; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Live prices: your browser requests public prices directly from Binance's public market-data service (data-api.binance.vision). Binance receives that request, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy. No account or personal data is involved.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the version date above shows the current one.
