About this experiment

This run holds a simple passive basket: the ten most traded eligible coins, sized by volatility, rebalanced once a day. It is a measurement control, not a strategy, and is not expected to beat anything. Its only purpose is to measure real execution costs and how often patient orders would fill, so that future research uses real numbers. Nothing here is a signal. Do not use it to trade.
