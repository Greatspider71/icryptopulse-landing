Before you continue

This website publishes a research experiment: simulated (paper) trades of a passive crypto basket, run only to measure trading costs.

  • It is not financial, investment or trading advice, and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
  • No real orders are placed and no money is managed. The basket is a measurement control, not a strategy.
  • Prices come from third parties and may be delayed or wrong.

Your agreement is remembered on this device only. See the Privacy Policy.
